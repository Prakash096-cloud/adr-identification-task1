Audit Checklist

  • Verify medicine name, strength, dose, route, start date, and last dose.
  • Record symptom onset, severity, duration, treatment, and outcome.
  • Review allergy history, prior exposure, concurrent medicines, and alternative causes.
  • Document missing information and follow local pharmacovigilance procedures.
  • Escalate urgently if symptoms suggest a severe allergic reaction.

Conclusion

Amoxicillin is the suspected medicine because symptoms were reported after treatment began. The available details are insufficient to confirm causality. A qualified healthcare professional should assess the patient and determine appropriate next steps.

Safety: Trouble breathing, swelling of the face/lips/tongue/throat, fainting, or rapidly worsening symptoms require emergency medical care.
